# Review — content-as-marketing doc set

> **Date**: 2026-08-19
> **Reviewed against**: live Directus dev (`dev-dashboard.swibba.com`), the repo at `develop` (Next 16.2.10), and current Google / AI-engine documentation
> **Docs reviewed**: `docs/content-as-marketing/` (16 files + `audits/slug-audit-baseline.md`), `scripts/marketing-content/` (13 files)

---

## Verdict

The plan is well-designed and badly grounded. The *architecture* is genuinely
good — the URL scheme, the polymorphic content store, the single shared
`getIndexationState` for page + sitemap, the facet shape cap, and the risk
register are all better than what most teams ship. The *premises* are wrong in
three ways that each independently stop the feature working, and they are all
verifiable in ten minutes against the live database.

The single biggest problem: **the plan gates everything on `status = 'published'`,
and 5,149 of 5,231 catalog nodes can never have that value.** `brands` is
`pending|approved|rejected`, `series` and `models` are `active|discontinued`.
Built as written, every brand, series and model URL — tiers 3–5, the entire
commercially valuable part of the tree — 404s.

The second: **the attribute-facet system in `15-facets-and-variants.md` has no data
behind it.** 99.93% of `models_attribute_values` rows have a NULL attribute FK,
and the two value columns are semantically inverted.

The third is strategic. The doc set's stated defence against a doorway-page
penalty is "live inventory data on every page." There are **8 published listings**
against **5,231 catalog nodes**. 18 nodes (0.34%) have any inventory at all; zero
nodes clear any supply threshold in the design. Meanwhile the indexation gate in
`06 §1` applies supply thresholds **only to modifier pages** — tiers 1–5 index on
word count alone. That combination is 36,617 AI-written pages with no inventory,
marked indexable, which is the precise shape of Google's
[scaled content abuse](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
policy.

**Recommendation: do not build phases 3–5 yet.** Phases 0–2 (schema, resolver,
server-rendered tiers 1–2) are worth shipping now and carry no policy risk. Fix
the status enums and the permission-policy bug first, add a supply floor to the
tier-page gate, and defer the AI backfill until inventory exists. See
[Recommended re-sequencing](#recommended-re-sequencing).

## Blockers

### B1 · `status = 'published'` 404s 98.4% of the catalog

**Doc**: `06-seo-structured-data.md §1` decision table line 32; `03-content-pipeline.md §2.1` step 1.

**Says**: entity absent or `status != published` → `notFound`; the generate flow fires when status is/becomes `published`.

**Actually**: three incompatible status vocabularies.

| Collection | Enum | Live distribution |
|---|---|---|
| `categories` | `draft \| published \| archived` | 11 published |
| `subcategories` | `draft \| published \| archived` | 71 published |
| `brands` | `pending \| approved \| rejected` | 80 approved, 130 pending, **0 published** |
| `series` | `active \| discontinued` | 1,056 active, **0 published** |
| `models` | `active \| discontinued` | 3,883 active, **0 published** |

**Evidence**: `GET /fields/{c}/status` → `meta.options.choices`; `GET /items/{c}?groupBy=status&aggregate[count]=id`. No doc in the set mentions `approved`, `active` or `discontinued`; `12-risks.md` never raises it.

**Impact**: 5,149 of 5,231 nodes 404. The generate flow fires for 82 nodes instead of 5,231. It also hides an unasked policy question: 130 of 210 brands are `pending` — unapproved, user-submitted — so a naive "treat approved+active as published" fix silently publishes 130 unmoderated brand pages.

**Fix**: one `PUBLISHED_STATUS` map in `src/features/catalog-content/lib/indexation.ts` — `{categories:['published'], subcategories:['published'], brands:['approved'], series:['active'], models:['active']}` — consumed by the resolver, the sitemap, the generate-flow filter and `08-check-slug-collisions.mjs`. Add an explicit decision in `06 §1` for `brands.status = 'pending'` (recommend: resolve, noindex, exclude from sitemap).

### B2 · The attribute-facet system has no data behind it

**Doc**: `15-facets-and-variants.md §4` lines 228-229 and 269-271; `03-content-pipeline.md §3` (`knownSpecs`).

**Says**: `models_attribute_values` holds normalised per-model values joined to `attribute_definitions`; `attribute_option_values.value` seeds from `models_attribute_values.value`; `knownSpecs` populates model-tier context.

**Actually**:
- 19,472 of 19,485 rows (**99.93%**) have `attribute_definitions_id = NULL`. Only 13 rows join, covering 3 models.
- The columns are **inverted**: `value` holds the attribute *name* (663 distinct pseudo-keys — `type`×2110, `ai_features`×771, `display`×707, `ram`×357, `storage`×391), and `available_options` holds the value as free text (7,522 distinct strings; 1,137 contain `/` or `,` — e.g. `"128/256GB"`, `"6.2 AMOLED 120Hz"`).
- Only 20 of the 663 pseudo-keys match any `attribute_definitions.name` or `.slug`.
- Of the 7 `is_variant` definitions (Processor, Size, Shoe Size, Mattress Size, RAM, Storage Capacity, Color), **six have zero rows**; Color has 2 models and 1 distinct value.

**Evidence**: `GET /items/models_attribute_values?aggregate[count]=id&filter[attribute_definitions_id][_null]=true` → `19472`; `[_nnull]=true` → `13`. Sample rows (ids 1421-1425): `{value:"ram", available_options:"8GB"}`, `{value:"storage", available_options:"128/256GB"}`.

**Impact**: `15 §10`'s worked example (`/iphone-13/256gb`, `/iphone-13/gold`) cannot be produced. The seeding union in `§4` step 1 would emit attribute *names* as option values, generating `/…/s24/ram` and `/…/s24/display`. `knownSpecs` is null for 3,881 of 3,883 models, so the prompt's `key_specs` rule and quality-gate check #15 never fire — silently removing the one genuinely model-specific input the prompt has. `§5`'s facet filter joins `listings_attribute_values.value`, a different table with 30 rows.

**Fix**: add a Phase-0 repair migration to `10-tasks.md` — map the 663 keys onto `attribute_definitions`, move `available_options` → `value` splitting compound strings, backfill the FK. Until it lands, `15-facets` must state that `seo_role: 'path'` cannot be granted to any attribute and the attribute dimension is dark.

### B3 · Authenticated permissions land on a policy almost no user holds

**Doc**: `02-data-model.md §8`; `scripts/marketing-content/02-apply-permissions.mjs:21-25`; `_directus.mjs` `resolvePolicies()`.

**Says**: the four `catalog_alerts` rows scoped `user_id = $CURRENT_USER` give signed-in users control of their alerts. The script resolves that policy as "first policy on `DIRECTUS_ROLE_DEFAULT`".

**Actually**: `DIRECTUS_ROLE_DEFAULT` is role "Default User", whose policy `cf17f4ca-…` is a **stub with 4 permissions held by 2 of 99 users**. Real users are moved off it at onboarding — [complete-profile/route.ts:71-74](app/api/auth/complete-profile/route.ts#L71-L74) assigns `INDIVIDUAL` or `BUSINESS_OWNER`.

| Policy | Users | Permissions |
|---|---|---|
| Individual User `4524307c` | 33 | 150 |
| Business Owner `978491f5` | 40 | 211 |
| Store Manager `247712e2` | 22 | 142 |
| Default `cf17f4ca` | **2** | **4** |

**Impact**: alerts are invisible to 95 of 99 accounts. Directus denies silently (empty result, not an error), so this presents as "alerts never show up" and gets chased in the frontend. The self-service unsubscribe path in `07-availability-alerts.md` is equally dead — a GDPR/CAN-SPAM-relevant broken promise.

**Fix**: emit one row per `(policy, collection, action)` over an `AUTHENTICATED_POLICIES` array covering all four real policies. Add a check to `07-verify-schema.mjs`.

### B4 · Tier 1–5 pages are indexable with zero inventory

**Doc**: `06-seo-structured-data.md §1` decision table, row 2; `03-content-pipeline.md §7` quality gate.

**Says**: `Catalog tier 1–5, published content for this locale, word_count ≥ 250, gate passed` → **index**. Supply thresholds appear only in the modifier rows.

**Actually**: the 17-check quality gate is entirely textual — JSON shape, word counts, language match, meta lengths, FAQ count, sibling trigram overlap, placeholder scan. **No check references supply.** Check #16 (price consistency) explicitly passes when `listingCount = 0`.

So a model page with **zero listings** and 300 AI-written words is `index, follow`, self-canonical, and in the sitemap. Across the catalog that is **5,231 nodes × 7 locales = 36,617 indexable pages backed by 8 published listings**.

`12-risks.md` R1 names this exact failure class as critical, but its mitigation table scopes supply gating to modifier pages only — the gap is not closed.

**Evidence** (live): 18 of 5,231 nodes (0.34%) have ≥1 published listing — 3/11 categories, 3/71 subcategories, 5/210 brands, 3/1,056 series, 4/3,883 models. Each covered model has exactly **one** listing, so **zero** nodes clear the ≥3 or ≥5 thresholds anywhere in the design. Derivable market vocabulary across all 8 listings: 3 cities (Cairo, Al Maragha, and `"gogog"` — test junk), 2 countries (one listing has `location_country=DZ` with `location_city=Cairo`), 1 currency, 4 conditions, 2 listing types.

**Policy context** ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)): *"Scaled content abuse is when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating Search rankings and not helping users… whether content is produced through automation, human efforts, or some combination."* The [March 2024 update](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2024/03/core-update-spam-policies) made the production method irrelevant — only the outcome counts. Note also that a word-count floor is not a quality signal Google recognises; check #2 (`≥ 250 words`) defends against nothing policy-relevant.

**Fix**: add a supply condition to the tier 1–5 index row. Concretely — tiers 1–2 index unconditionally (82 nodes, real navigational value); tiers 3–5 require `listing_count ≥ 1` on the node **or** its subtree; everything else renders `noindex, follow`. This is a two-line change to the decision table and one predicate in `indexation.ts`, and it is the difference between 36,617 indexable pages and ~18.

### B5 · Documented prod-rehearsal commands silently target dev

**Doc**: `11-verification.md §14`; `02-data-model.md §9`.

**Says**: rehearse on a prod clone with `--env` / `--snapshot`.

**Actually**: no script in `scripts/marketing-content/` parses either flag. `arg()` in `_directus.mjs:46-49` silently drops unknown flags, and target selection is exclusively `NEXT_PUBLIC_DIRECTUS_URL` read from `.env.local` (`_directus.mjs:21-34`).

**Impact**: an operator following `§14` verbatim applies the schema to **dev** while believing they are rehearsing against a clone, and the banner confirms nothing is wrong.

**Fix**: implement `--env=<name>` in `_directus.mjs` (resolve URL+token from `.env.<name>`, abort if the resolved host does not match), or rewrite `§14` to the `NEXT_PUBLIC_DIRECTUS_URL=<clone> node …` form `DATABASE-CHANGES.md §9` already uses.

### B6 · The AI-prompt seed step the verifier hard-fails on cannot work

**Doc**: `scripts/marketing-content/README.md:75-76,161`; `07-verify-schema.mjs §6b:121-134`.

**Says**: seed `ai_settings.system_prompt_catalog_content` via `scripts/seed-ai-settings-data.mjs`.

**Actually**: that script seeds a fixed literal of exactly four prompts (`vision`, `match`, `attributes`, `enrich`) at `:105-111`. `grep -c catalog scripts/seed-ai-settings-data.mjs` → **0**. There is no `DEFAULT_CATALOG_CONTENT_PROMPT` in `src/lib/ai/prompts.ts` — it exists only as prose in `03-content-pipeline.md §4`.

**Impact**: `07-verify-schema.mjs` hard-fails at step 6b with no way to satisfy it. This matters more than it looks because of the known `getAiSettings()` merge behaviour — once the singleton holds a value, editing `prompts.ts` is inert at runtime. `03 §4` documents that trap correctly and then the tooling cannot execute it.

**Fix**: add `DEFAULT_CATALOG_CONTENT_PROMPT` to `prompts.ts` and `system_prompt_catalog_content: extractPrompt(promptsSrc, 'DEFAULT_CATALOG_CONTENT_PROMPT')` to the `prompts` object in `seed-ai-settings-data.mjs`.

### B7 · `listing_type` is a 7-value compound enum

**Doc**: `01-url-architecture.md §3:124` and `§7` (3-row table); `05-page-composition.md §4`; `15 §5` filter block.

**Says**: `listingType?: 'sell' | 'rent' | 'swap'`, filtered with `_eq`.

**Actually**: live choices are `sell, rent, swap, sell_or_swap, sell_or_rent, rent_or_swap, all` — four are compound. The codebase already has a helper for exactly this: [rentalAvailability.ts:23](src/shared/lib/rentalAvailability.ts#L23) defines `RENTABLE_TYPES = new Set(['rent','sell_or_rent','rent_or_swap','all'])`. `src/features/listings/types/index.ts:8` carries a wider comma-joined variant list.

**Impact**: every `/for-rent`, `/for-sale`, `/for-swap` page and every `listing_count` undercounts — a listing marked `sell_or_rent` appears on neither the sale nor the rent page. Since counts feed the supply thresholds *and* the indexation gate *and* the sitemap, the undercount propagates everywhere.

**Fix**: `LISTING_TYPE_MEMBERSHIP: Record<'sell'|'rent'|'swap', string[]>` in `modifier-vocab.ts` mirroring `rentalAvailability.ts`; use `_in`, never `_eq`.

**1 — Fix the four things that make the plan unbuildable (before any code).**
`PUBLISHED_STATUS` per tier (B1) · authenticated-policy fan-out (B3) · `--env` or the explicit-URL form (B5) · `sort=id` in `getAll()` then regenerate the slug baseline (Q1). Roughly a day. Nothing below works without these.

**2 — Ship phases 0–2 as scoped. 82 pages × 7 locales = 574 URLs.**
Tiers 1–2 only: 11 categories + 71 subcategories. These are real navigational pages with genuine search intent, they replace a `"use client"` shell that crawlers see as empty, and they carry **zero** scaled-content risk at this volume. This alone fixes the biggest problem `00 §1` identifies. Includes the resolver, `/catalog/` routing, metadata, breadcrumbs, the `[subcat]/metadata.ts` canonical fix, and 308s from `/marketplace/categories/*`.

**3 — Add the supply floor to the tier gate (B4) before tiers 3–5 exist.**
Two lines in the `06 §1` decision table. Tiers 3–5 index only with `listing_count ≥ 1` on the node or its subtree. Today that yields **18 indexable nodes**, which is the correct answer for 8 listings — and it scales automatically as inventory grows, with no further decisions.

**4 — Catalog data cleanup, which is the real blocker on everything after.**
Merge/archive the 84 duplicate models (Q2) · repair `models_attribute_values` (B2 — 19,472 rows) · purge the `"gogog"` city and the DZ/Cairo listing (M4) · decide the currency question on `models` (Q3). Until B2 is repaired, `15-facets-and-variants.md` should be marked **blocked**, not scheduled.

**5 — AI backfill only for nodes with inventory.**
At 18 qualifying nodes that is 126 calls, not 36,617. Add a Phase-3 entry condition to `10-tasks.md`: do not backfill a tier until ≥N of its nodes carry ≥3 published listings. This makes spend track inventory automatically and keeps the corpus defensible under the scaled-content policy — the pages that exist are the pages with something real behind them.

**6 — Defer phases 4–5 (modifiers, facets, sitemap index) until there is supply.**
With 0 nodes clearing any threshold, every modifier page would be `noindex` on day one. A sitemap index for 574 URLs is premature; the current flat sitemap handles 50k.

**7 — Rescope the GEO work.**
Fix the `llms.txt` 404 bug (G3) and update the bot names (G2) — both are ~20-line changes worth doing now. Drop the llms.txt catalog-tree rebuild from phase 5 (G1). Add AI-referral segmentation to `00 §4` (G5).

The honest framing: **this feature's bottleneck is inventory, not content.** 8 published listings is the number that decides everything else. Steps 1–3 are worth doing regardless because they fix real bugs and unlock server rendering. Steps 5–6 should wait.
